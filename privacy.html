Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Pretense values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our website, platform, and services.

Introduction

Pretense (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard information when you use our website, platform, and services. By accessing or using Pretense, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect information such as your name, email address, organization details, billing information, and account credentials when you register or interact with our services. In addition, we may collect technical and usage-related information including device details, browser type, IP address, log data, API metadata, and feature usage analytics to help improve performance, reliability, and security.

How We Use Information

Pretense uses collected information to operate and improve our services, maintain platform security, provide customer support, process payments, monitor usage, and prevent unauthorized access or abuse. Usage data may also help us better understand how users interact with our platform so we can continue improving the product experience.

Code & Data Protection

Protecting sensitive code and proprietary data is a core part of our platform design. Pretense uses Code Protection to transform sensitive identifiers before code is processed through AI systems. Whenever possible, original mappings remain local to your environment. We do not intentionally store proprietary source code beyond what is necessary to provide the service, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

Data Sharing

We share limited data with providers that help us operate our infrastructure, process payments, deliver support, or maintain security and analytics services. These providers are contractually required to protect your information and only process data as necessary to perform their services. The two advertising and visitor-identification tags described below are different: LinkedIn and RB2B use what they collect for their own purposes, so we treat both as “sharing” personal information and honour opt-outs from it.

Advertising Cookies and Your Opt-Out

Two third-party tags, the LinkedIn Insight Tag and RB2B, load on this website only after you click Accept on the cookie banner. These scripts may set cookies or similar identifiers. LinkedIn uses the LinkedIn Insight Tag to recognise your browser on other websites and to measure and target LinkedIn ads. RB2B states that it can identify the companies, and for some US visitors the individuals, who visit a website. Under California law (CCPA/CPRA) we treat both tags as “sharing” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If you click Decline, neither tag loads.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as your request to opt out of the sale and sharing of your personal information. LinkedIn Insight and RB2B then never load, even if you clicked Accept on an earlier visit, and the cookie banner does not ask you again. A GPC signal or Decline opts you out of both tags.

Changing your choice: the cookie banner does not reappear after you click Accept or Decline, and this website does not yet offer a separate cookie-settings control. To withdraw an Accept, turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser, or clear this website’s stored data in your browser so that the banner asks you again. To undo a Decline, clear this website’s stored data; while GPC is on, the tags stay off. Clearing this website’s stored data also signs you out of your Pretense account. It does not remove cookies that LinkedIn has already set on its own domain; delete those in your browser’s cookie settings or through LinkedIn.

Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information, including encryption, access controls, secure infrastructure practices, and monitoring systems. While we work hard to maintain strong security standards, no method of transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain operational integrity. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and applicable legal requirements.

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights related to your personal information, including the ability to access, correct, delete, or request portability of your data. You may also object to certain processing activities or request additional information about how your data is handled.
