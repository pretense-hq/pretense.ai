Cookie Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Pretense uses cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, enhance user experience, analyze performance, and maintain platform security across our services.

Introduction

Pretense uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience, analyze website performance, maintain security, and support essential platform functionality. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and the choices you have regarding their use.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites recognize your browser, remember preferences, and improve functionality and performance across sessions.

How We Use Cookies

Pretense uses cookies to provide core website functionality, maintain secure sessions, remember user preferences, analyze usage patterns, and improve the overall performance of our services. Some cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our platform so we can optimize the user experience and identify areas for improvement.

Types of Cookies We Use

We may use essential cookies required for the operation of the website, performance and analytics cookies to measure traffic and usage, functional cookies to remember settings and preferences, and security-related cookies to help protect accounts and prevent unauthorized access.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be provided by third-party services such as analytics providers, infrastructure services, or embedded integrations. These third parties may collect information in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies. LinkedIn Insight and RB2B load only after you click Accept on the cookie banner, and these scripts may set cookies or similar identifiers. If you click Decline, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, neither loads.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality, performance, or availability of some features within the Pretense platform. The cookie banner does not reappear after you choose, and this website does not yet offer a separate cookie-settings control. To change your choice, clear this website’s stored data in your browser so that the banner asks you again, or turn on Global Privacy Control to keep LinkedIn Insight and RB2B off. Clearing this website’s stored data also signs you out of your Pretense account, and it does not remove cookies that LinkedIn has already set on its own domain; delete those in your browser’s cookie settings or through LinkedIn.

Data & Privacy

Information collected through cookies may include browser type, device information, pages visited, session activity, and general usage analytics. After you click Accept, the LinkedIn Insight Tag sets a LinkedIn cookie that LinkedIn uses to recognise your browser on other websites for advertising, so it does track you across third-party sites. RB2B states that it can identify the companies, and for some US visitors the individuals, who visit a website. Under California law we treat both tags as “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising, and a GPC signal or Decline opts you out of both. Our Privacy Policy explains how to opt out, including with Global Privacy Control.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our services. Continued use of our website after updates become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
